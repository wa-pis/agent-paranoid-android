# Change: compatibility-surface-inventory

## Why

Retained aliases and wrappers are tested but do not have one documented status,
canonical replacement, or minimum support window.

## What Changes

- Inventory CLI aliases, Python wrappers, and transitional model access.
- Mark each retained surface supported and name its canonical replacement.
- Define the deprecation and removal window.

## Impact

No compatibility surface is removed or behaviorally changed.

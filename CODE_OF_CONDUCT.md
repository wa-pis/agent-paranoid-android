# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Contributors
should be constructive, assume good intent, accept correction, and focus
discussion on the work. Examples, reports, and reviews must protect private
information and use synthetic data.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, personal attacks, publishing private information, or deliberately
disrupting project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, community
spaces, and public events where someone represents the project.

## Reporting And Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `onepis2word@gmail.com`. Do not include
production data, raw PII, credentials, or unrelated private information. The
maintainer will review reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and
may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or permanently ban
participants according to the severity and pattern of behavior.

Good-faith reporters and participants in an investigation will not face
retaliation. Knowingly false reports or retaliation are themselves violations.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and the Python Community
Code of Conduct, adapted for this project's small-maintainer structure and
sensitive-data safety requirements.
